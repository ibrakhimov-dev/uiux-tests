@1
Q: What is usability testing?
A: The designer clicks through the app alone, looking for errors
B: Asking users for their opinion through a survey
C: A facilitator gives tasks and watches the participant do them
D: Asking an AI how convenient the app is
W: The facilitator asks the participant to do tasks in the product, watches what they do and listens to what they say.
O: A — designers don't get lost in their own app. B — a survey asks for opinions and doesn't see actions. D — an AI can't actually use the app.

B: The volunteer student isn't good at using phones yet

D: A complex pattern is always safer than a simple one
W: Designers are the same: they never get lost in their own app. That's why the design is tested by someone who doesn't know it.
