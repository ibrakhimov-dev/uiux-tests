@1
Q: What is a prototype?
A: A clickable sample that isn't coded yet
B: A finished site that is coded and online
C: A colored, finished screen image you can't click
D: A chart of links between pages and sections
W: There's no code behind it — only connections between screens.
O: B is a finished product. C is a mockup; you can't click it. D is a sitemap.
