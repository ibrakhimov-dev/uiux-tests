@1
Q: What is a wireframe?
A: The page's skeleton: what goes where, how much space, how it works
B: The page's finished look: colors, fonts and images
C: The page's code: HTML, CSS and JavaScript files
D: A list of pages: all the sections in the sitemap
W: A wireframe has no color, fonts, images or decoration — only structure.
O: B is a mockup. C is the developer's job. D is the sitemap.
@2
Q: What is the purpose of a wireframe?
A: For the team to agree on colors
B: For the client to approve a nice picture
C: For the team to agree on the structure
D: For the developer to start coding right away
W: The designer, developer and client agree on the structure, not on the looks.
O: A and B: color and looks come at a later stage. D: code is written after the structure is agreed.
@3
Q: Why is a wireframe drawn in gray?
A: Because gray looks modern and beautiful
B: So attention goes to structure, not color
C: Because Figma doesn't allow other colors
D: Because it's cheaper to print
W: With color, people start arguing "make the button blue".
O: A: the goal isn't beauty. C: Figma has every color. D: wireframes are made on screen.
